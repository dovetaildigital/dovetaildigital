Cookie policy

At Dovetail Digital, we value your privacy and strive to make your experience with us as transparent as possible. This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website, dovetaildigital.co.uk, and how they help enhance your browsing experience.

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you don’t agree with our use of cookies, you can manage your cookie preferences or disable cookies in your browser settings.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, improve user experience, and collect anonymous data for analytics purposes. Cookies can be “session” cookies, which are deleted once you close your browser, or “persistent” cookies, which remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

2. Types of cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies on our website:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the basic functionality of the website. Without these cookies, parts of the website might not work as intended.
  • Performance cookies: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous data on website usage. For example, they allow us to see which pages are most visited, how users navigate the site, and identify any errors or problems. This data helps us improve the performance of our site.
  • Functional cookies: These cookies allow the website to remember your preferences and settings (such as language or region) to provide a more personalized experience.
  • Targeting/advertising cookies: These cookies track your browsing habits to deliver targeted ads that are relevant to you. They are often placed by third-party advertising networks and help measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.

3. How we use cookies

Cookies on our website help us:

  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Analyse website traffic and trends to optimise site performance.
  • Provide tailored content and advertisements to enhance your browsing experience.
  • Remember your preferences, such as language or region, to ensure consistent use across sessions.

4. Third-party cookies

We may allow trusted third-party services to place cookies on our website to assist with analytics, advertising, or social media functionality. These services include Google Analytics and social media platforms such as Facebook and X. These third parties may use their own cookies, and we encourage you to review their privacy and cookie policies.

5. Managing cookies

You have the ability to control cookies in your browser settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, but please note that doing so may affect your user experience and limit certain features of the website.

To manage your cookies:

  • Google Chrome: Go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Go to Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Go to Settings > Cookies and site permissions.
  • Safari: Go to Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.

For more detailed instructions, please refer to the help section of your browser.

We may update our cookie policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the revised policy will take effect immediately upon posting. We encourage you to check this page regularly for the latest information.
