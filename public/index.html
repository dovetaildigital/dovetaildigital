ABOUT DOVETAIL DIGITAL

Experience for
causes that matter.

I’m James Holloway, and Dovetail Digital is where I bring together over 18 years of experience in digital marketing, fundraising and communications within the charity sector.

I’ve spent my career helping causes tell their stories clearly and raise the funds they need to do their work, across digital campaigns, donor communications and fundraising strategy. Dovetail Digital is the space where that experience is available on a freelance basis.

A THOUGHTFUL, PRACTICAL APPROACH

Support that
fits your charity.

Every charity has a different story and a different set of constraints: budget, team size, cause. I work alongside in-house teams, bringing an outside perspective without losing sight of what makes an organisation distinctive.

With nearly two decades across the sector, I’m just as happy talking things through in an advisory capacity as I am delivering the work myself. And having worked across organisations of very different sizes, I know that a small charity with a tight budget needs a different kind of support to a large one—and I’m just as keen to help either way.
