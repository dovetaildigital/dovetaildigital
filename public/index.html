About Dovetail Digital

Hi, I’m James Holloway. Dovetail Digital is where I bring together more than 18 years of experience working with charities. My work has covered fundraising, digital marketing and communications, from planning campaigns to improving the systems charities use to understand their results.

I offer freelance support on specific projects or on an ongoing basis, working alongside your team and shaping the support around your goals, capacity and budget.
