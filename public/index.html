About
James Holloway.

I’m James Holloway, and Dovetail Digital is where I bring together over 18 years of experience in digital marketing, fundraising and communications within the charity sector.

I’ve spent my career helping charities explain their work and raise money through digital campaigns, donor communications and fundraising strategy. I now offer that experience on a freelance basis through Dovetail Digital.
